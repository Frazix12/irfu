# Theme: **Monsoon Instrument**

### Concept
A quiet design laboratory after Dhaka rain: cool mineral surfaces, translucent components, precision markings, and one restrained ruby “calibration” accent. The identity feels engineered yet tactile—closer to a crafted electronic instrument than a conventional developer portfolio.

### Mood
- Calm, intelligent, quietly experimental
- Product-design precision with architectural depth
- Cool daylight rather than dark mode or editorial warmth
- Technical without becoming cyberpunk

### Palette
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Mineral canvas | `#E8EEE9` |
| Porcelain surface | `#F7FAF7` |
| Deep green-black text | `#18221F` |
| Monsoon green | `#355B50` |
| Brushed aluminium | `#AEBAB7` |
| Lichen secondary | `#B8C79F` |
| Calibration ruby | `#A83243` |
| Fine rules | `#CAD4CF` |

Ruby appears only on the primary CTA, active states, and one cube detail—never as a broad background.

### Typography
**Instrument Sans Variable** throughout, avoiding a generic sans/mono split.

- Hero title: wide, slightly tightened, 72–104px
- Body: 17–19px with generous line height
- Metadata: uppercase, narrow tracking, 11–12px
- Large project numbers used as faint structural graphics
- No code-style typography, terminal motifs, or excessive pills

### Hero Composition
An asymmetric 12-column “display plinth”:

- Left third: compact identity block
  - `IRFAN HOSSAIN`
  - `Full-stack developer — screen to circuit.`
  - **Hire Me ↗** linking directly to email
  - Small `Dhaka, Bangladesh` coordinate label
- Right two-thirds: the Zepa Cube Unicorn scene, oversized and partially crossing the grid
- A thin ruby calibration line aligns the CTA with the cube’s center
- Capability labels—`WEB / SYSTEMS / MOBILE / HARDWARE`—sit around the scene like restrained instrument annotations

No introductory paragraph or résumé summary in the hero.

### Cube Treatment
The **Zepa Cube Unicorn WebGL** scene is presented as a digital prototype under glass, not a decorative background.

- Transparent scene over the mineral canvas
- Cube occupies roughly 52–58vw and slightly crops at the right edge
- Cool celadon reflections, graphite edges, and one ruby internal face
- A soft elliptical shadow beneath creates physical weight
- Pointer movement produces slow rotational resistance, like handling a dense object
- Surrounding labels remain static while the cube moves
- On reduced-motion devices, hold a strong three-quarter view with the same shadow and annotations

### Project-Card System
Cards resemble precision sample trays: squared silhouettes, 18px corner radii, fine aluminium borders, and inset image wells.

1. **BTEB Flow** — full-width flagship card  
2. **AmarHisab** — broad application card  
3. **SMD Rework Station** + **DIY Soldering Station** — separate equal cards placed directly adjacent, forming a hardware pair  

Each card contains only:

- Project number
- Name
- One-line outcome
- 2–3 discipline labels
- Image or restrained product render
- Small diagonal detail arrow

Software cards use clean interface crops. Hardware cards use close macro photography, annotated with tiny component callouts. Hovering lifts the inner image plate by 6px while the outer tray stays fixed.

### Motion
- Slow cube drift; direct but damped pointer response
- Sections reveal through 12–18px vertical settling and opacity
- Project images move independently inside stationary frames
- Ruby calibration marks slide into alignment on hover
- No glowing cursors, particle fields, marquee text, or aggressive parallax

### Why It Fits Irfan
**Monsoon Instrument** connects Irfan’s web, backend, mobile, and electronics work through one idea: building complete, usable systems. Its cool Dhaka-rain atmosphere gives the portfolio a personal sense of place without cultural cliché, while the calibrated product language presents him equally well to freelance clients, technical collaborators, and employers.
