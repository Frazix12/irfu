## Theme — **Delta Instrument**

**Mood**  
A Dhaka field-engineering notebook meets a precision electronics bench: structured, tactile, calm, and technically confident—never cyberpunk or corporate SaaS.

**Palette**
- Drafting paper — `#E8E7E1`
- Carbon ink — `#17201E`
- Deep delta green — `#17483F`
- Anodized blue — `#496B8A`
- Instrument yellow — `#D4B942`
- Zinc linework — `#A9AEA8`
- Clean white — `#F7F7F3`

Use drafting paper as the dominant surface, carbon for type, green for structural blocks, blue sparingly for interactive states, and yellow only as a calibration marker.

**Typography**
- Headlines: condensed industrial grotesk, uppercase, tightly set
- Body/CTA: neutral contemporary sans
- Metadata: restrained technical mono
- Oversized section numbers, coordinate labels, measurement ticks, and thin ruled annotations

**Hero composition**
A strict 12-column drafting grid:
- Left 5 columns: `IRFAN HOSSAIN`, one-line role, location, and **Hire Me →**
- Center 5 columns: tall “instrument chamber” containing the cube
- Right 2 columns: four vertically indexed fields—Web Products, Systems, Mobile, Hardware
- Small baseline annotation: `DHAKA / STUDENT + FREELANCE DEVELOPER`

The chamber slightly breaks the grid, creating one deliberate moment of visual tension.

**Zepa Cube treatment**
Treat the Unicorn WebGL scene as a live engineering specimen—not a background. Place it inside a borderless cutout with corner registration marks, a quiet coordinate crosshair, and one status label: `SYSTEM / ACTIVE`. Let the cube retain depth and luminosity while surrounding surfaces remain matte. Its hues should lean deep green, mineral blue, silver, and pale yellow—no purple/cyan glow. Pointer movement produces subtle parallax; the scene never follows the cursor aggressively.

**Project-card system**
Cards resemble modular equipment panels:
- **BTEB Flow:** full-width flagship panel with large project number and interface crop
- **AmarHisab:** medium horizontal panel with ledger-like ruled structure
- **SMD Rework Station** + **DIY Soldering Station:** equal adjacent cards sharing one alignment rail, but clearly separate projects
- Each card contains only: index, title, field, one short outcome, and `VIEW →`
- Category is expressed through structure and diagrams—not technology badges

**Motion**
- Grid lines draw in once on entry
- Section numbers lock into place like calibrated readouts
- Cards reveal through vertical masking, not fades
- Hover shifts registration marks by 2–3px and exposes one annotation
- Cube motion remains fluid while the interface motion stays precise and mechanical

**Why it fits Irfan**
“Delta Instrument” connects Dhaka’s geographic identity with Irfan’s unusually broad field practice—from web systems and databases to microcontrollers and electronics. It presents him as someone who builds complete working systems, not merely someone with a list of tools.
